ONE LINE. A LITTLE WONDER.

Make a living loop.

  1. Start on the glowing ring.
  2. Move up, down, left or right to connect neighboring spaces.
  3. Visit every flower, then return to the ring.
  4. Your path cannot cross itself or pass through a stone.

Drag with a mouse or finger, or tap one space at a time. Use arrow keys after choosing a space. Backspace undoes a step. Escape closes a window.

Shorter loops earn more stars. Hints show one possible route; you can always find your own.
