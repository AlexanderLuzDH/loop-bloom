Keep a way home.

  1. Begin at the ring. Each connection costs one move.
  2. Visit the numbered flowers in order: 1, 2, 3…
  3. Return to the ring in exactly the displayed number of moves.
  4. Move up, down, left or right. No crossing, revisiting spaces or passing through stones.

Drag or tap. Arrow keys move; Backspace undoes. Undo is unlimited. There is no timer.

One hint reveals the next move on a route that still works, or tells you how far to undo. A solve with hints is marked assisted. Complete it without hints to earn the mastery mark.
